## Scoring guidance

- Use the organization's approved risk methodology if one exists.
- Do not invent numeric probability or financial impact estimates when evidence is unavailable.
- Mark unknown inputs as **Unknown** and explain what evidence is needed.
- Critical/high risks require an explicit treatment owner and escalation; they are not silently accepted by an automated workflow.
- Residual-risk acceptance requires an authorized human decision under P policy. A workstream author or automated evaluator cannot approve its own exception.
- Closing a risk requires evidence that the trigger is removed or controls are effective; elapsed time alone is not closure.
